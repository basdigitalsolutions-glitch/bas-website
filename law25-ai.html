Law 25 and AI for Quebec manufacturers

Plan how information is handled before you automate incoming requests.

Questions to resolve before a pilot

Before adding AI to intake, identify the personal information in emails, signatures and attachments. A drawing can be confidential even when it contains no personal information.

Plan data handling first

The Commission d'accès à l'information describes privacy impact assessments for certain projects and for disclosures outside Quebec. List authorized people, providers, storage, retention and deletion; start with redacted samples; and have the person responsible for the protection of personal information approve the rules.

A general “Law 25 compliant” claim does not replace this assessment. This guide is not legal advice.

Source: Commission d'accès à l'information, main changes under Law 25
