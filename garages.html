Questions, answered straight

Do I need a credit card to start the 30-day free trial?

No. Send your business name and email to request the trial. A paid plan ($49 CAD per month) starts only if you confirm it after the 30 days.

Will the assistant invent repair estimates or promise a bay time?

No. It uses only the services, prices and hours you approve, and passes anything else to your team. Your team confirms every appointment request.

How do we update seasonal prices or holiday hours?

Send us the change by email and we update your assistant. There is no dashboard to learn.

How is customer information handled under Quebec’s Law 25?

We collect only what is needed to pass a request to your team, and the assistant is clearly presented as an AI assistant. See our Law 25 and AI page and our privacy notice.
