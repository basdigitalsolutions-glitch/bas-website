Your questions, answered

Does it book appointments by itself?

No. It collects appointment requests and emails them to you. Your team confirms every appointment.

What if it doesn’t know an answer?

It gives the driver your garage’s phone number so they can contact your team.

Can it quote repairs?

Only the prices you approve, like a tire change or an oil change. For repairs, it collects the request and your team gives the quote.

Does it work on my current website?

Yes. Wix, WordPress, Squarespace and other websites that allow custom scripts use one line of code. I handle setup. Any platform permissions or plan restrictions are checked before installation.

Is it Law 25 compliant?

Conversations are processed to generate replies. We help you add a line to your privacy policy explaining this use. This alone does not establish Law 25 compliance. Before launch, we review the personal information collected, service providers, retention and your garage’s responsibilities with you.
