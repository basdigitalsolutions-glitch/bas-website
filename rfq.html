The Industrial RFQ Pilot

Phase 1 · Map

We map your process and agree on one request type, expected fields, and review criteria. Together, we select the sample requests and outputs to check.

Phase 2 · Build and test

We build and test the workflow using the agreed sample requests. Your team reviews the outputs, flags gaps, and confirms the changes within the agreed scope before handoff.

What you get

Structured intake, a missing-information checklist, and a human review step. Handoff includes test results, known limitations, and workflow documentation.

Scope, timeline and price are confirmed in your proposal after assessment. No ongoing commitment. The schedule starts once scope, access, and samples are agreed. No revenue guarantees. No production ERP writes until you approve.

Any discount and permission to describe results are agreed separately. No case study, including an anonymized one, is published without your approval.

Questions before you start

Do we have to change our ERP?

Not necessarily. We assess your current tools, interfaces, and access first. The pilot can start in a separate review queue. Any integration, including Dynamics 365, depends on that assessment.

Who sees our drawings?

Do not send confidential drawings through the public form. Before sharing them, we agree on authorized people, providers, storage locations, and retention. Use redacted samples for the pilot. Before processing personal information, we assess the agreed providers, access, storage locations and retention. We do not promise Quebec-only hosting. Where personal information is involved, the Law 25 assessment, including before a transfer outside Quebec, is part of project preparation.

Are quotes sent automatically?

No. Your estimator reviews and approves a quote before it is sent. The workflow flags gaps; it does not make commercial commitments for you.
