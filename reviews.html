Google reviews

Answer every Google review in French and English, without losing an evening to it.

How it works

  • A review comes in

    BAS watches your Google Business Profile, so a new review does not sit unanswered while you are with a client.

  • A draft appears

    Written in the language of the review, in natural French or English. It only refers to what the client wrote.

  • You approve in one tap

    You get an email with a link. Read the draft, edit it if you like, then approve. The reply is posted only then.

What a draft looks like

Fictional examples. Each draft answers in the language of the review.

  • 5 stars, in French

    Review

    Superbe balayage ! L’équipe a pris le temps de comprendre ce que je voulais. Je recommande à 100 %.

    Draft reply

    Merci beaucoup, Sophie ! Nous sommes ravies que le balayage corresponde à ce que vous aviez en tête. Au plaisir de vous revoir.

    Review

  • 1 star, flagged for you

    Review

    The colour is not what I expected. I showed a reference and it came out much darker. I’m disappointed.

    Flagged for the owner: we suggest a private follow-up before any public reply.

    Draft reply

    Thank you for telling us, Priya. We take this seriously and would like to understand what happened. Please contact the salon directly so we can talk it through.

Built-in safeguards

  • Every reply needs your approval by default.
  • Reviews of 3 stars or less, complaints, threats and sensitive topics are flagged for you.
  • Replies never offer discounts or refunds, and never include phone numbers or links.
  • Text inside a review cannot give instructions to the system.
  • Posting automatically for positive reviews is optional, and only if you ask for it.

Questions, answered straight

Does it post replies without me?

No, not by default. Each reply waits for your approval. Automatic posting for clean 4 and 5 star reviews is optional and only turned on if you ask.

Do you need my Google password?

No. You add BAS as a Manager on your Google Business Profile, and you can remove that access at any time.

What about a bad review?

It is flagged for you with a calm draft, and we suggest contacting the client privately first. Nothing is posted until you decide.

What if the review is in another language?

It is flagged for you, and the draft is written in both French and English.
