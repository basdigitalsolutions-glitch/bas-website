Questions, answered straight

Do I have to replace my booking tool?

No. The assistant does not book on its own. It collects the request and your team confirms it in whatever tool you already use.

Will the assistant quote a final price for colour?

No. It shares only the starting prices you approve. Colour consultations and corrections go to your stylist for the final price.

Do I need a credit card to start the 30-day free trial?

No. Send your business name and email to request the trial. A paid plan ($49 CAD per month for your first 12 paid months at the Founding rate, then $69 CAD per month) starts only if you confirm it after the 30 days.

How is client information handled under Quebec’s Law 25?

We collect only what is needed to pass a request to your team, and the assistant is clearly presented as an AI assistant. See our Law 25 and AI page and our privacy notice.
