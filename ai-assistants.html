AI assistants

A bilingual assistant that answers customers and keeps your team in control.

Your website assistant answers routine questions in English and French using information you approve, collects requests, and hands the details to your team.

Request a personalized demo

What it does

  • Answers routine questions

    Hours, location, services, starting prices and policies, in English and French, from information you approve.

  • Collects appointment requests

    Gathers the service, contact details and preferred time. Your team confirms.

  • Collects quote requests

    Gathers what your team needs to assess an inquiry and prepare a response.

  • Routes the details

    Sends request details to email or an agreed system after assessment and set-up.

  • Hands over to your team

    Passes uncertain questions, exceptions, assessed prices and commitments to staff.

Your team confirms appointments, approves quotes and handles commitments. The assistant does not book or promise on its own.

Business automation

Repeated admin work, handled the same way every time, with your team deciding what matters.

  • Inquiry intake

    Collect the details your team needs from forms and chat.

  • Request routing

    Send request details to email, a customer tracker or an agreed system, after assessment and set-up.

  • Integration assessment

    Before promising anything, we assess how this would connect to tools such as ERP, Outlook, Teams or a CRM.

Where people stay in charge: your team reviews and approves requests, quotes and commitments. Nothing is written to your business systems until you approve.
