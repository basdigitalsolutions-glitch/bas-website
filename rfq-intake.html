How to automate RFQ intake

A practical starting point for manufacturers in Montreal and Quebec.

English & French · Human approval on every quote · Scope and price agreed before we build

One focused first workflow

Start with one recurring request type. Gather a few redacted examples, including an incomplete request and a revised drawing.

Define the required information

For each request, list the customer, part, quantity, material, revision, drawing, and requested date. Separate information received from information inferred: uncertainty must stay visible.

Design for exceptions

A missing drawing, duplicate, or new revision should trigger human review. Test these cases before expanding the workflow. Check who can access the documents, too.

Keep an approval step

Prepare a draft record in a review queue. The estimator validates the information and quote before sending. Measure corrections and time spent at each stage. Those observations guide the next phase; they do not guarantee a commercial outcome.
