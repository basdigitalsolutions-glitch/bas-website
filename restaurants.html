Questions, answered straight

Does the assistant speak English and French?

Yes. It detects the customer’s language and replies in English or Québec French, using the information you approve.

Does it confirm reservations?

No. It collects the date, time, party size and contact details and sends the request to your team. Your team confirms with the customer.

Does it process payments?

No. It does not take payments or complete orders. If you already have online ordering, it shares that link.

Can it answer questions about menu prices?

Yes, from the menu and prices you approve. If a price is not on the list, it says so and passes the question to your team.

How does it receive menu updates?

Send us the changes by email and we update the assistant.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The assistant and this page are designed for phones, and customers can open it from a QR code.

Can it connect to an existing ordering system?

It can link customers to your existing online ordering page. A direct connection to ordering or point-of-sale systems is not available yet.

What happens when it cannot answer?

It tells the customer it does not have that information and offers to take their name and contact details so your team can reply.

How much setup is required?

We set it up with you using your menu, prices and hours, and you review everything before it goes live. There is no setup fee.

What happens after the free trial?

The 30-day trial needs no credit card. A paid plan only starts if you confirm it, at $49 CAD per month. Otherwise the trial simply ends.

How is customer information handled?

We collect only what a request needs, tell visitors they are talking to an AI assistant, and handle deletion requests as described in our privacy notice. Read the privacy notice and Law 25 page for details.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime, with no setup fee and no contract.
