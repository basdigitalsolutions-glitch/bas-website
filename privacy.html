Privacy notice and third-party tools

Last updated November 19, 2026. This page explains what BAS Digital Solutions collects, which third-party tools process it, and how to make a request under Quebec’s Law 25.

What we collect

Inquiry form. When you submit an inquiry, the form provider collects the answers you enter: your name and contact details, optional company information, service and project details, language preference, and consent to contact you about the inquiry. BAS uses this information to review your request, reply, and manage follow-up.

Free-trial request form on this website. The trial form asks for your business name, work email and website address, which we use to prepare and start your trial.

Demo chats. The demo conversations use a fictional business. Please do not type confidential or sensitive information into them.

Types of third-party service that process this information

  • Form provider: hosts the inquiry form and collects your answers.
  • Form-forwarding service: forwards the free-trial request form from this website to BAS by email.
  • Website hosting: hosts this website. Hosting providers can log technical data such as IP address and the pages requested.

BAS also uses email, spreadsheet and AI assistant tools internally to review and follow up on your inquiry. To know the name of each provider, email info@basdigitalsolutions.ca.

Where information may be stored

Some of these providers may process or store information outside Quebec and outside Canada, including in the United States and other countries where they operate. Your information may then be subject to the laws of those places. This notice does not promise storage in Quebec or a fixed deletion period. For the names of providers, details about where your information is held, or to ask BAS to limit what it sends to these tools, email info@basdigitalsolutions.ca.

Privacy Officer

BAS Digital Solutions is founded and operated by Bandar Sharkh, who acts as the person responsible for the protection of personal information (Law 25). Title: Founder, BAS Digital Solutions, Pierrefonds-Roxboro (Montreal), Quebec.

Contact: info@basdigitalsolutions.ca with the subject “Privacy request.”

Your requests

To ask what information BAS holds about you, request access or correction, ask for deletion, or ask BAS to stop follow-up, email info@basdigitalsolutions.ca with the subject “Privacy request.” Describe your request without sending identity documents or confidential files. BAS will review your request and explain the next steps and any retention limits.

Files and confidential information

Use the public form for an initial description only. Do not upload or paste confidential RFQs, drawings, passwords, or payment details. Agree on sample content and a suitable sharing method with BAS before sending files.
