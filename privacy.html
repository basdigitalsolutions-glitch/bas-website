Privacy notice

Privacy notice and third-party tools

Last updated November 19, 2026. This page explains what BAS Digital Solutions collects, which third-party tools process it, and how to make a request under Quebec’s Law 25.

What we collect

Inquiry form (Tally). When you submit an inquiry, Tally collects the answers you enter: your name and contact details, optional company information, service and project details, language preference, and consent to contact you about the inquiry. BAS uses this information to review your request, reply, and manage follow-up.

Free-trial request form on this website. The trial form asks for your business name, work email and website address, which we use to prepare and start your trial.

Demo chats. The demo conversations use a fictional business. Please do not type confidential or sensitive information into them.

Third-party tools that process this information

  • Tally hosts the inquiry form and collects your answers.
  • ChatGPT is used by BAS for assisted review of inquiries.
  • Google Sheets holds a sales tracker with relevant inquiry and follow-up records.
  • Gmail is checked by the inquiry monitor for notifications and replies.
  • WHC hosts the public business mailbox, info@basdigitalsolutions.ca.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Hosting providers can log technical data such as IP address and the pages requested.
  • FormSubmit forwards the free-trial request form from this website to BAS by email.
  • Google Fonts serves the website font, which means Google can receive your IP address when a page loads.

These services process inquiry information for these tasks. This notice does not promise storage in Quebec or a fixed deletion period.

Your requests

To ask what information BAS holds about you, request access or correction, ask for deletion, or ask BAS to stop follow-up, email info@basdigitalsolutions.ca with the subject “Privacy request.” Describe your request without sending identity documents or confidential files. BAS will review your request and explain the next steps and any retention limits.

Files and confidential information

Use the public form for an initial description only. Do not upload or paste confidential RFQs, drawings, passwords, or payment details. Agree on sample content and a suitable sharing method with BAS before sending files.

See also Law 25 and industrial AI in Quebec and the Terms of Service.
