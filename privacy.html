Privacy policy

How we handle personal information.

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Person responsible for personal information

Bandar Sharkh, Founder, BAS Digital Solutions. Contact: info@basdigitalsolutions.ca.

What we collect

When you submit the inquiry form or email us, we collect your name, contact details, company and the project description you provide. Do not send passwords, payment details or confidential drawings through the public form.

Why we use it

To review and answer your request, discuss fit and scope, and send a proposal. We do not sell personal information.

Service providers and storage

We use these providers: Tally for the inquiry form (Tally reports storage in Europe); Web Hosting Canada for our email (Canada); GitHub Pages to host this website (United States), which keeps basic technical logs such as IP addresses for security; and Google tools to keep track of inquiries (data may be stored in the United States or elsewhere). Your information may therefore be stored outside Quebec.

Retention

We keep inquiry information for 24 months after our last contact with you, unless you become a client or a longer period is agreed for an active project. We then delete or anonymize it.

Your rights

You may ask to access or correct your information, ask that it be deleted, or withdraw your consent, by emailing info@basdigitalsolutions.ca. You may also contact the Commission d'accès à l'information.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. The embedded Tally form may set its own cookies when you use it.

Project data

Before you share drawings or other project data, we agree in writing on who may access it, which providers are used, where it is stored and how long it is kept.
