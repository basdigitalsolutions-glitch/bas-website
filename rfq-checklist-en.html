RFQ readiness checklist

A starting point for your free 30-minute call.

  • Choose one recurring request type
  • Identify intake emails and forms
  • List part, quantity, and material requirements
  • Define the authoritative drawing and revision
  • Capture the requested delivery date
  • Plan for incomplete requests and duplicates
  • Name the reviewer and approver
  • Identify tools: ERP, Outlook, Teams, or CRM
  • Check access, storage, and retention
  • Prepare redacted sample requests
  • Agree scope, price, and acceptance criteria
