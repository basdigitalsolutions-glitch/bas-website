Industrial manufacturers & suppliers · Dedicated RFQ service

The 2-Week Industrial RFQ Pilot

Week 1 · Map

We map your process and agree on one request type, expected fields, and review criteria. Together, we select the sample requests and outputs to check.

Week 2 · Build and test

We build and test the workflow using the agreed sample requests. Your team reviews the outputs, flags gaps, and confirms the changes within the agreed scope before handoff.

What you get

Structured intake, a missing-information checklist, and a human review step. Handoff includes test results, known limitations, and workflow documentation.

Fixed price confirmed in your proposal. No ongoing commitment. The schedule starts once scope, access, and samples are agreed. No revenue guarantees. No production ERP writes until you approve.

Any discount and permission to describe results are agreed separately. No case study, including an anonymized one, is published without your approval.

What sample requests should you prepare?

Use a fictional or redacted example of a typical request: email text, requested part or service, quantity, revision, material, and timing. Note any missing information and the output your team expects to review. We agree on suitable examples during the audit.

Do not attach or paste samples into the public inquiry form. Describe your need only. Before any file sharing, BAS and your team agree on what to redact, the transfer method, authorized recipients, and retention arrangements. No confidential drawings are needed for the initial audit.
