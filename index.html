Privacy & personal information

When you submit an inquiry, Tally collects the answers you enter: your name and contact details, optional company information, service and project details, language preference, and consent to contact you about the inquiry. BAS uses this information to review your request, reply, and manage follow-up.

BAS uses ChatGPT to assist with reviewing inquiries and Google Sheets to keep relevant inquiry and follow-up records. A scheduled inquiry monitor is configured to check Tally submissions and Gmail for notifications and replies. BAS’s public business email address is info@basdigitalsolutions.ca. These services process inquiry information for these tasks; this notice does not promise storage in Quebec or a fixed deletion period.

To ask what information BAS holds about you, request access or correction, ask for deletion, or ask BAS to stop follow-up, email info@basdigitalsolutions.ca with the subject “Privacy request.” Describe your request without sending identity documents or confidential files. BAS will review your request and explain the next steps and any retention limits.

Use the public form for an initial description only. Do not upload or paste confidential RFQs, drawings, passwords, or payment details. Agree on sample content and a suitable sharing method with BAS before sending files.
