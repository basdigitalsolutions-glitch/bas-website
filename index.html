    Common questions

    Everything to know before you start.

    How much does it cost?

    The website AI assistant is free for 30 days, with no setup fee and cancel anytime. The Founding rate is $49 CAD per month for your first 12 paid months for the first 5 businesses; the standard price is $69 CAD per month. Other channels such as WhatsApp or phone, and website refreshes, are quoted separately.

    Can it say things I have not approved?

    No. It works only from the information you give us, such as services, hours and prices, and you review and approve it before launch. The chats in our demos are scripted examples.

    Does it work in French and English?

    Yes. It answers in the language the customer writes in, and you can review both languages before launch.

    Can it book appointments?

    It collects appointment requests and passes them to your team. It does not book into your calendar on its own. Calendar integrations are assessed separately.

    What happens to my customers’ information?

    We collect only what is submitted, use it to answer the request, and process it with third-party tools. Read our privacy notice and our Law 25 page.

    Can I stop during the trial?

    Yes. You can cancel anytime, including during the 30 days.
