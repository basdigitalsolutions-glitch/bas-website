Questions before you start

Do we have to change our ERP?

Not necessarily. We assess your current tools, interfaces and access first. A pilot can start in a separate review queue. Any integration, including Dynamics 365, depends on that assessment.

Are quotes sent automatically?

No. Your estimator reviews and approves every quote before it is sent. The workflow flags gaps; it does not make commercial commitments for you.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope, applications and ongoing support. The pilot has a fixed price in your proposal, and you always receive a proposal before work begins. Third-party subscriptions are agreed separately.

Do you guarantee revenue or time savings?

No. We agree on a measurable objective and test the workflow. Results depend on your process, data quality, adoption and external tools.

Who sees our drawings?

Do not send confidential drawings through the public form. Before any project data is shared, we agree on who can access it, which providers are used, where it is stored and how long it is kept. Redacted samples are used for the pilot.
